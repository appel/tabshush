Sync between devices

Keeps your snoozed tabs in Other Bookmarks › TabShush, so Firefox Sync carries them to your other devices. This needs Firefox Sync with Bookmarks ticked, and TabShush on each device. When you snooze, you choose which devices may wake the tab; it wakes on the one you are using. An idle device waits 15 minutes first, so the device you are at goes first.

    Shortcuts

    Click a shortcut and press the keys you want: Ctrl or Alt together with a letter, number or function key, optionally with Shift. Snooze shortcuts act on the current tab, or on all selected tabs. If a shortcut is already used by Firefox or another add-on, the other one may win.

    About

    Heads up: TabShush was made with AI. I understand not everyone's on board with that, hence this disclosure.

    TabShush

    Listen, I don't know about you, but I'll readily admit I have a bit of a tab hoarding problem. I'll come across something cool, say an upcoming Ben Affleck movie, and I'll be like "Nice! I want to go check out that Ben Affleck movie when it comes out 3 months from now." But then I can't be arsed to add it to my calender. So what do I do? I use the SnoozeTabs add-on to snooze that puppy until release week comes a-knockin'.

    SnoozeTabs has been great. But there hasn't been an update for it for few years. In fact, 1.1.0 was released on Feb 22, 2018. The dev owes you and me didly squad, of course, but there are a few UX things that irk me, like the time picker. And the lack of dark mode. And how it stands out like a sore thumb in dark mode. And one crappy day it will snooze its last tab and stop working.

    So I've toyed with the idea of forking it, but I'm not an extension developer. But with AI, for better or worse, that's a little less of a concern. Heck, people are out there cloning freakin' PhotoShop. So I thought "why not have AI build this add-on for me?" And, as it happens, for you!

    Ladies and gentleman, I bring you TabShush.

    Not ready for a tab just yet? Give it a little shush! TabShush tucks it away and brings it back right when you want it: later today, tomorrow, this weekend, the next time Firefox opens, or whenever you like. When a tab wakes up, it pops in next to the one you're on with a gently ringing bell, so it never sneaks past you. Keep your snoozed tabs in bookmarks and they'll even follow you to your other devices.

    Import from SnoozeTabs

    Extensions cannot read each other's data, so this takes one copy and paste:

    1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in a new tab.
    2. At SnoozeTabs, click Inspect and open the Console tab.
    3. Paste this line there and press Enter. It copies SnoozeTabs' data to the clipboard: copy(JSON.stringify(await browser.storage.local.get()))
    4. Paste the result below.

    Firefox may ask you to type "allow pasting" in the console first; that is a safety check for pasting code. SnoozeTabs keeps its own copy, so you can remove it once the import looks right.
